# Oikoumenē: Dynamic Place Names for Crusader Kings III

Oikoumenē expands CK3's dynamic title names. The same county, duchy, kingdom or empire will now appear under different names. Each language in the mod is made for the ** whole playable map**  before I release it. Old Norse is not limited to Scandinavia, Persian to Iran, or Old English to Britain.

**Current version:** v0.2 – “Orlando”

## What it adds

- **Dynamic title names** across the whole playable map
- **Historical names and exonyms** wherever they are known
- **Reconstructed names** for places where no historical form survives
- **Full support** for English, French, German, Polish, Russian and Spanish versions of the game

## How names are chosen

Where I can, I use names that are actually attested in medieval sources, including historical (not modern!) exonyms.

For places a culture never historically reached, there is obviously no medieval source to copy. There I work from the language itself – its historical spelling and sounds, its morphology, and the way it normally forms place names – and reconstruct a form that could plausibly have entered ordinary use.

## Corrections and contributions

If you find a typo, a broken name, a missing form, an inconsistent spelling, or know a better historical or linguistic solution, please open an Issue.

Sources are especially useful when you have them.
